# Duoverse Discord App Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 8, 2026**

AlNoMansLand operates the Duoverse Discord app. This policy covers the bot and Duoverse features used through Discord. Contact [duoverseoffficial@gmail.com](mailto:duoverseoffficial@gmail.com) for privacy questions or requests.

## Information we use

- **Commands and replies.** Discord sends the account identifier, selected command, input, and relevant server, channel, message, or interaction identifiers. We use this information to respond, apply permissions, and prevent duplicate actions.
- **Linked companion features.** If you link Duoverse, we use your Discord account identifier and the relevant Duoverse account, pairing, approved device, permission, and connection records to verify access. Where you have enabled sharing, the companion can show availability and current game activity and carry out actions you choose, such as sending a nudge. When a Discord sign-in is needed, it happens on Discord; the app does not ask for your Discord password.
- **Media utilities, where enabled.** When you run a media command, choose a message in the Apps menu, or submit media in a server's configured GIF intake channel, we process the selected or submitted files and relevant attachment details. Depending on the tool, this can include image, video, audio, emoji, stickers, captions you enter, or the information needed to identify custom emoji in a selected message. A server manager enables automatic intake. The worker filters server events to the configured intake before processing media.

## Use and sharing

We use this information to run the features you request, enforce permissions, deliver responses, and diagnose or prevent abuse. Linked actions can reach your approved Duoverse device or paired partner as the feature requires. Command results are sent as private Discord replies. Automatic GIF results are posted in the configured `#gifs` channel, where members with access to that channel can see them. Duoverse attempts to remove a forwarded intake message only after Discord confirms that the result was posted.

Discord receives information needed to process commands and deliver messages and files. Supabase hosts the Duoverse backend. Where media utilities are enabled, the processing worker and its hosting or HTTPS relay providers handle the information needed to perform and return the conversion. GitHub hosts these policy pages under its [Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). We do not sell Discord data, use it for targeted advertising, or use Discord message content to train AI. We may disclose information when legally required.

## Retention and your choices

Media files are processed temporarily. Unfinished media requests expire, and the worker removes their queued data as it runs. The worker keeps identifiers needed to route GIFs and avoid posting a duplicate. Linked account, device and permission information is kept while needed to provide the linked features. We delete Discord data when it is no longer needed or on a valid deletion request, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

Email [duoverseoffficial@gmail.com](mailto:duoverseoffficial@gmail.com) to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your Duoverse Discord data. Include your Discord username and which data you mean. We may ask you to verify that you control the account. Please keep passwords, tokens and recovery keys out of the request.

You can remove the app from your Discord account, or have a server manager remove it from a server or revoke its channel access. Removing or disconnecting the app stops the relevant access; contact us to request deletion of existing records. Messages and attachments on Discord, and copies other people have saved, are subject to Discord's and the recipients' controls. See [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).
