How Duoverse uses information to connect your phone, your Windows PC and your partner—and the controls you have over it.

Sharing you can manage

What this covers

Duoverse for iPhone and iPad includes local creative tools and a companion connection to your existing Duoverse Windows account and paired partner. Real connected setup currently uses an invitation and approval on your PC. The ordinary Explore demo uses clearly labelled fictional data.

The public developer name is AlNoMansLand. Privacy and support contact: duoverseoffficial@gmail.com.

Information used by connected features

The information used depends on the features you enable and the content you choose to share.

  • Account and connection information: account and partner identifiers, approved-device identifiers, public encryption keys, connection permissions and account/profile information such as a name or portrait.
  • Shared content: the messages, notes, lists, calendar information, work schedules, drawings, recordings, media and transfers you create or send through supported features.
  • Partner status: permitted presence, game or media activity, and shared location or timezone information used by the app, widgets and Watch companion.
  • Delivery information: notification preferences, push-registration identifiers and the connection records needed to route notifications, widgets and approved requests.
  • Support information: the details you voluntarily include when you contact support. Please avoid sending private messages, passwords, recovery keys or device tokens.

Connected services also process request and security information needed to authenticate requests, protect the service, diagnose errors and keep approved connections working.

What happens on your device

Make GIF, Edit Picture and the Photos Convert to GIF action work with the media you select locally. Using these tools does not require a paired PC. Saving an output to Photos or Files, copying it, or choosing a destination in the share sheet is your action.

If you send content through a connected feature—such as Quick Drop or a picture to your PC clipboard—the selected content is transmitted for that request. The destination app or service you choose may have its own privacy practices.

With your permission, the camera can scan a pairing code and the microphone can record a voice nudge or a partner recording. iOS permission settings control that access. Local preferences and caches support features such as saved drawings, recent details and media previews.

Who information is shared with

Duoverse routes permitted shared information to your paired partner and approved devices. A PC action still needs the relevant permission and an available approved PC. The selected app catalogue can include names, store labels and icons; executable paths and launch arguments remain on the PC.

Supabase provides Duoverse’s connected cloud account, database, storage and service infrastructure. The primary cloud region is Singapore. Apple handles push delivery and platform features such as Photos and the share sheet. Discord provides the linked account identity and account-ownership verification used by the connected experience. GitHub hosts the public Windows downloads and these information pages. Optional content integrations may retrieve public artwork or information from the relevant provider.

A primary cloud region is not a promise that every provider processes every request in that country. The services you choose when sharing an output have their own privacy practices.

GitHub Pages records visitor IP addresses for security when you open these information pages, whether or not you are signed in to GitHub. These pages do not add analytics scripts, tracking pixels or a contact form.

Third-party services process requests under their own privacy practices. You can read the Supabase, Apple, Discord and GitHub privacy information. An integration you enable or a destination you choose may involve additional providers.

Encryption, notifications and widgets

Duoverse uses authenticated encryption for supported shared content and phone transport. Account identifiers, permissions, routing and other service metadata are still processed by the service; encryption does not mean that no information is collected or that every field is end-to-end encrypted.

Notification registration connects an approved phone to its delivery preferences. Alert content depends on the category: nudge alerts use generic notification text, while supported activity alerts can include an already shared game title. Do not rely on notification privacy settings as a substitute for the app’s sharing controls.

Widgets use a limited read-only connection and local shared cache. They cannot authorize PC commands. They may show permitted partner details, including status and time, on a Home Screen, Lock Screen or Watch surface. Review those sharing and lock-screen privacy choices before enabling them.

Updates depend on iOS scheduling, connectivity and the freshness of the shared status. The clock can advance locally even when status updates are delayed.

Your choices

  • Use the local tools without connecting a PC, or explore the fictional demo.
  • Review approved phones and their permissions on your PC, and disconnect a phone when you no longer want it connected.
  • Manage the available partner-sharing, widget, notification and sound settings.
  • Change camera, microphone, Photos and notification permissions in iOS settings.
  • Choose what to save or share, and contact support about your privacy questions.

Disconnecting a phone and ending a pairing are different from deleting the underlying Duoverse account.

Your information and deletion requests

For questions about your information, or to request access, correction or deletion, email duoverseoffficial@gmail.com. We may need to verify account ownership and confirm the affected shared space before acting on a request.

Deleting an account and disconnecting a phone are separate actions. Account deletion can end pairing and affect the hosted shared space for both partners. If that space is closed and removed, its shared messages, nudges, notes, calendar events, lists, upload records and transfer history include contributions from both people.

Your partner’s separate account, profile, personal settings and data outside the affected space are distinct from that shared history. Files already saved separately on either person’s devices are also separate; removing a hosted copy cannot remove copies someone has independently saved.

A request does not cause an immediate automatic deletion through this page. The scope, verification and any applicable retention requirements need to be addressed as part of handling the request. Ask us if you need information about the status or effect of a particular request.

Retention and policy updates

Information is retained according to its purpose: maintaining your account and shared space, completing requested deliveries, supporting enabled features, resolving support or security issues, and meeting applicable legal obligations. Shared history and account records have different lifetimes from status caches and temporary deliveries, which use the relevant feature’s expiry and cleanup controls.

Removing the app does not, by itself, delete your Duoverse account or the hosted shared space. Changes to live service records are separate from operational logs and backups. Copies in those systems may remain until their normal retention or expiry, or where preservation is required for a legal or security reason. We do not promise that every copy is erased immediately after a request.

You can contact us for information about retention relevant to your request. When this policy changes, the effective date on this page will be updated. Material changes affecting the use of your information will be explained through an appropriate notice.

Questions about your information?

Email duoverseoffficial@gmail.com. Include your app version and a brief description of the question. Do not include passwords, encryption recovery keys, signing files or private device tokens.
