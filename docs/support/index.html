Start with what you need

Use the phone tools

Open Tools to make a GIF or edit a picture. These tools work locally without pairing a computer. You can also try Explore demo from Home.

Connect your shared space

For connected partner features and PC controls, link your phone to your existing Duoverse account using the PC invitation and approval flow.

Your PC connection

How do I connect my phone?

Install the official Duoverse Windows companion, sign in and use its phone-connection invitation. On your phone, choose Connect my phone and scan the code or enter the invitation code. Compare the security numbers and approve the phone on the PC.

Get the Windows app from the official public downloads. Keep the app updated before troubleshooting a connection.

Does my PC need to stay on?

PC controls, launching apps and receiving a request on that PC need an available approved computer running Duoverse. Duoverse does not wake an offline PC. Local editing tools work without a PC.

Connected data and notification delivery also depend on a valid approved connection, your sharing choices, connectivity and the relevant devices or services being available.

Can I try the app before pairing?

Yes. Tools works without pairing. Home → Explore demo → Just demo shows fictional partner and shared-space data. Simulated demo actions do not send real nudges or control a real computer.

A PC request is waiting or failed

Check that the intended PC is online, running Duoverse, and still approves this phone and the requested action. Confirm that the target app is installed and approved on that PC. A disconnected or revoked connection needs ordinary pairing and approval again.

Pictures, GIFs and recordings

Where are my edited pictures and GIFs saved?

Choose Save or Share in the tool. The result goes to the destination you select, such as Photos, Files or another app. Editing alone does not send the media to your partner or PC.

If the receiving app displays a GIF as a still image, save the GIF file and check that you are sharing the GIF output rather than a preview image.

Can I convert a picture inside Photos?

The Duoverse Convert to GIF action works from the system share sheet for supported selected media. Conversion and saving take place in the action’s interface. Access to the available action and permission prompts is controlled by iOS.

The microphone or camera is unavailable

Check Duoverse’s permissions in iOS Settings. Microphone access is needed for voice nudges and recordings; camera access is needed to scan a pairing code. Close any active recording or call if the microphone is busy, then try again in the latest Duoverse version.

If the app closes unexpectedly, send the app/build number, iOS version and what you tapped. Share a crash report only after checking it for private information.

Notifications, widgets and Watch

I did not receive an alert

Check iOS notification permission, Focus and sound settings, then Duoverse’s notification category and pause settings. The partner must share the relevant activity, and the underlying connected setup must still be valid.

Silent sound choices do not play an audible cue. iOS controls final presentation, including when a notification appears on Apple Watch.

My widget’s status has not changed

Open Duoverse and check that the partner and connection details are correct. Review partner/widget sharing and privacy choices. A locally advancing clock does not mean that new activity data has arrived.

iOS schedules widget updates. Connectivity, stale data and privacy changes can affect what is shown; Duoverse does not promise a fixed two-second refresh interval.

The Watch companion is not current

Check that the iPhone companion is installed, connected and up to date, and that your Watch can reach the phone. Shared details depend on the same account, permissions and data freshness. Use the Watch app on iPhone to check installation and notification settings.

Privacy and account controls

Review the available sharing controls in Duoverse, phone permissions on your PC and app permissions in iOS Settings. See the privacy policy for the data used by the connected features.

What is the difference between disconnecting and deleting?

Disconnecting removes the approved phone connection. It does not, by itself, delete the account or the hosted shared history. Account deletion is a separate permanent flow.

How can I ask about deleting my information?

Email duoverseoffficial@gmail.com with a brief description of your request. Account ownership and the affected shared-space scope may need to be verified before a change can be made. Do not send your password, recovery key or pairing code.

Account deletion can affect the hosted shared space for both partners, including both people’s shared history. The partner’s separate account and data outside that space are distinct. Files already saved separately on either person’s device remain there.

A support request does not trigger an immediate automatic deletion. See the privacy policy for the distinction between disconnecting, shared history and deletion requests.

Contact support safely

Email duoverseoffficial@gmail.com with:

  • Your Duoverse version/build, device model and operating-system version.
  • The feature you used and the steps leading to the issue.
  • An optional screenshot with private names, content and codes removed.
Keep private information private. Never send passwords, encryption recovery keys, Apple signing files, pairing codes, device tokens or unreviewed personal logs. Support does not need them to begin investigating.
